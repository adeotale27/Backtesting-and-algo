jodi

v1.0 · CONTINUOUS THETA HARVESTING
Jodi is a NIFTY option-selling strategy that continuously creates fresh short strangles ("Jodis") whenever conditions allow. It is not an iron condor held to expiry — completed Jodis are immediately replaced with new ones so theta is always working for the portfolio while risk stays strictly bounded by per-leg SL, kept hedges, and a daily 1 % loss circuit-breaker.

Trade flow

Wake up (Mon/Tue @ 9:30 · Fri @ 15:15)→ Pick strikes per premium rules→ Create Jodi (2 shorts + 2 hedges) · size = equity ÷ ₹2L lots
↓
Harvest theta→ On SL: exit that short (₹SL × 1.02)→ Wait 10-15 min→ Repair (≤ 2 times)
↓
Book profit when surviving short ≤ 20% entry→ Close hedges→ Try new Jodi
↓
On Tue @ 15:20 → force-exit everything
